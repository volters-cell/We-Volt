    EU Tracker is an open-data project: every record is a JSON file in the repository, and every record carries its source. Roll-call votes come from the European Parliament's own record, published on its open data portal, which names every member and how they voted. Boundaries are simplified for display and imply no position on any border. Where the data comes from, and what is missing.
